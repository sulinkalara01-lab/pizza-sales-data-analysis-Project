Executive Summary, Key Findings, Recommendations
🍕 Pizza Sales Performance & Business Intelligence Analysis

📌 Executive Summary

This analysis evaluates overall sales performance, customer ordering patterns, and product category contributions to optimize operational efficiency and maximize revenue. The data reveals clear demand cycles, high-performing product segments, and opportunities for menu optimization.

🔍 Key Findings & Conclusions

    Peak Demand Windows: 
1) Hourly Patterns: Order volume spikes significantly during lunch hours (12:00 PM – 1:00 PM) and dinner peaks (5:00 PM – 7:00 PM).
2) Daily Volume: Demand peaks towards the end of the week (Thursdays through Saturdays), with Fridays consistently driving the highest total order counts.   

Sales Mix by Category & Size:

     Category Contribution: 
  1) The Classic and Supreme categories lead both overall volume and total revenue, while Veggie and Chicken maintain stable niche demand.   
2) Size Preference: Large (L) pizzas drive the dominant share of revenue, followed by Medium (M), indicating that customers strongly prefer sharing and family-sized options.

       Product Performance Extremes:
1) Top Sellers: Pizzas such as the Classic Deluxe, Barbecue Chicken, and Hawaiian consistently rank among the top 5 by units sold.
2) Underperformers: Specialty recipes (such as the Brie Carre Pizza) sit at the bottom 5 in sales volume, reflecting low consumer interest or limited product awareness.

   💡 Strategic Recommendations

       Labor & Kitchen Scheduling:
Align kitchen prep and front-of-house staffing with peak rush periods (12:00 PM – 1:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 7:30 PM), especially on Thursday through Saturday, to reduce ticket times and improve customer satisfaction.

    Promotions & Average Order Value (AOV) Boost:
Implement targeted meal bundles (e.g., Large Pizza + Side + Beverage) during off-peak weekday hours (Monday to Wednesday, 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM) to smooth demand curves and elevate the Average Order Value.

    Menu Engineering & Inventory Rationalization:
Capitalize on Winners: Feature top-selling pizzas prominently in marketing campaigns and prime spots on the menu/app interface.   
Re-evaluate Bottom Sellers: Review or reformulate bottom-performing items (e.g., Brie Carre) to reduce spoilage of low-turnover ingredients, or replace them with seasonal specials.  

    Targeted Category Marketing:
Create focused promotions for the Chicken and Veggie categories to capture health-conscious or alternative dietary preferences without diluting the core Classic/Supreme sales.
